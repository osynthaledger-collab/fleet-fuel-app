From the pump to the books in three steps.

No paper slips to hand in at month end, and no retyping numbers into a spreadsheet.

  1. Photograph the slip

    Take a photo at the pump, or choose one from the gallery. The photo stays attached to the entry.

  2. Check what the app read

    Pump, date, liters, price, total and receipt number are filled in for you. Correct anything that looks wrong.

  3. Save and send

    The entry reaches the office the moment you save it. Nothing more to submit.

Questions

Why does my phone warn me before installing?

The app is installed from a file instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm. Allow it for your browser once, then tap Install.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. This download is for Android phones only.

I do not have a login.

Your fleet manager creates the accounts. Ask them for your email and password.

Do I need internet to use it?

Yes. Slips are uploaded and read on our server, so you need mobile data or Wi-Fi when you add an entry.
